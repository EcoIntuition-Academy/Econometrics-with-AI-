Core Foundations

What is Econometrics?

The quantitative measurement of economic relationships combining theoretical logic, empirical data, and rigorous statistical inference.

Economic Theory

What relationship should exist? Provides behavioral logic, agent optimization, market equilibrium, and foundational causal hypotheses.

Data

What do we actually observe? Captures real-world decisions across cross-sectional surveys, macroeconomic time series, and micro-panel datasets.

Estimation

What does the evidence tell us? Quantifies structural parameters through OLS, Instrumental Variables, Maximum Likelihood, and robust estimators.

Inference

How confident are we in the conclusion? Evaluates sampling uncertainty, tests hypotheses, assesses standard errors, and validates identification assumptions.

Economic Theory + Mathematics + Statistics + Data → Evidence-Based Economic Conclusions
Educational Methodology

The Econometric Thinking Flow

How this course trains you to approach empirical economics with first-principles scientific rigor.

1 Ask
What economic question are we trying to answer?

Identify the core mechanism, policy dilemma, or behavioral causal relationship under investigation.

2 Model
How should the relationship be represented?

Specify functional forms, dependent variables, control sets, and stochastic error properties.

3 Estimate
What parameters does the data imply?

Calculate sample coefficients using OLS, Instrumental Variables, Fixed Effects, or Maximum Likelihood.

4 Test
How uncertain are our estimates?

Compute sampling distributions, standard errors, t-statistics, and joint hypothesis restrictions.

5 Diagnose
Are our assumptions failing?

Stress-test for heteroskedasticity, serial correlation, omitted confounders, and instrument weakness.

6 Interpret
What does the result actually mean economically?

Translate parameters into marginal effects, elasticities, counterfactual predictions, and policy impacts.

Pedagogical Framework

How AI Supports Your Learning

Econometrics is the core discipline; generative AI acts as an active intellectual coach to deepen understanding, not bypass it.

Visual Intuition

Explore difficult econometric concepts visually—from multidimensional projection geometry to sampling distributions and residual envelopes.

Ask Why

Use guided AI prompts to investigate assumptions, probe identifying restrictions, and understand why specific estimators behave differently.

Interpret Results

Practice translating raw statistical outputs into precise economic statements, elasticities, and policy impacts without falling into causal fallacies.

Experiment

Change assumptions and parameters dynamically—simulate omitted variable bias, measurement error, or heteroskedasticity and observe the consequences live.

Core Pedagogical Principle: AI supports econometric reasoning — it does not replace it.
